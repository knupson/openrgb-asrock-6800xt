Add ASRock RX 6800 XT Phantom Gaming GPU RGB support

Registers the Navi 21 ASRock Phantom Gaming card (1002:73BF, subsystem
1849:5202) with the existing ASRock GPU SMBus controller and makes the
detector usable on Windows.

Two changes were needed beyond registering the PCI IDs:

* The presence check used i2c_smbus_write_quick(). The Windows AMD ADL
  backend cannot issue I2C_SMBUS_QUICK and returns -1 unconditionally, so
  the detector could never fire there. A short pure I2C read is used
  instead; it is answered by the RGB controller at 0x36 and NAKed by every
  other address on the GPU bus, verified by scanning 0x08..0x77 on the ADL
  bus of an RX 6800 XT Phantom Gaming.

* This card's firmware does not implement the 0x14 channel query. The
  response is a rolling transaction counter, so the channel bitmap reads
  back as zero and detection was rejected. When the query yields nothing,
  the detector now falls back to a per-subsystem-device channel layout.
  Hardware testing showed only channel 6 (Top Side) drives LEDs on this
  card; channels 3 and 7 are accepted but light nothing.

Colour control over command 0x10 was confirmed working on the hardware
before and after the change.
